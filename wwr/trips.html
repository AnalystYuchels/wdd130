Explore Our Rafting Trips

1. Family River Adventure

Perfect for beginners, families, and groups who want to experience rafting at a comfortable pace. Enjoy gentle rapids, scenic river views, and guidance from an experienced trip leader.

  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Best for: Families and first-timers
  • Includes: Raft, paddle, life jacket, helmet, and guide
Ask About This Trip

2. Scenic River Explorer

Take a longer journey through beautiful natural scenery. This trip combines gentle river sections with occasional splashes, making it a great choice for friends and outdoor lovers who want both relaxation and adventure.

  • Difficulty: Easy to moderate
  • Duration: 3–4 hours
  • Best for: Friends and nature lovers
  • Includes: Safety equipment, guide, and scheduled rest stops
Ask About This Trip

3. Extreme Rapids Challenge

Ready for a bigger challenge? Work as a team to navigate stronger rapids with an experienced guide. This adventure is designed for confident participants who want an energetic day on the water.

  • Difficulty: Advanced
  • Duration: 4–5 hours
  • Best for: Experienced adventurers
  • Includes: Safety equipment, specialist briefing, and professional guide
